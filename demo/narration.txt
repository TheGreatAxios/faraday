This is Faraday. When a hospital runs an MRI or CT scan, they can't upload sensitive patient data to cloud AI.

Faraday solves this by running the analysis directly in the doctor's browser.

The AI agent gets real tools: it can inspect scan dimensions, measure high-intensity tumor regions, center the crosshair, and switch to three-D rendering.

And when it's time to export findings, it requires human approval.

The doctor gets instant measurements. The patient scan never leaves the room.

Faraday. Private, agent-native medical imaging.
